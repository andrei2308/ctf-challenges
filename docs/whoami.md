## How the writeups are written

Each writeup follows the actual solve, not a cleaned-up version of it. That means the decisions and pivots, the tooling and environment setup, the intermediate observations, and the constraints that shaped the final exploit. The goal is a pattern you can reuse on the next similar challenge, not just a flag.

## Scope

Only retired challenges, explicitly shareable content, or competition material where publication is allowed. Everything here is for educational use.
